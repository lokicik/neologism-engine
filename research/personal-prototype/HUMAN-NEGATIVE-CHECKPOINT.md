# Phase 305 absolute human gate: negative checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-24

## Decision

The personal phonographic-prototype architecture fails the frozen absolute
human gate and is closed. It must not enter production shadowing, Auto,
`generate()`, WASM, web types, storage, taste, or a better-name claim.

No anchor, candidate, source mapping, response, feature, weight, threshold, or
gate changed after the user collection was opened. Two fresh audits reproduced
report and manifest byte-for-byte.

## Result

- Collection structure passed: 30/30 decisions, 24 primaries, six repeats,
  twelve ratings per arm.
- Concealed repeat consistency was only `3/6`, below the frozen `5/6` gate.
- Prototype arm: two `Kullanırım`, zero `Belki`, ten `Hayır`; non-rejection
  `2/12`, mean ordinal `0.3333`.
- Phase 303 control arm: two `Kullanırım`, two `Belki`, eight `Hayır`;
  non-rejection `4/12`, mean ordinal `0.5000`.
- Paired by brief: two prototype wins, seven ties, three losses. Required wins
  were at least seven and losses at most two.
- Prototype use count, non-rejection count, non-rejection uplift, paired wins,
  paired losses, and repeat consistency all failed.

The prototype `Kullanırım` names were `Blodow` and `Gampag`. Control
non-rejections were `Boarce`, `Prefo`, `Nessway`, and `Pytoo`. Because the
repeat audit changed `Bastork` from `Hayır` to `Kullanırım`, `Mectice` from
`Hayır` to `Belki`, and `Pytoo` from `Belki` to `Kullanırım`, these individual
labels are too unstable and sparse to train another spelling scorer.

## Interpretation and next boundary

The automatic development and sealed passes were real mechanism results, but
they optimized abstract articulatory resemblance inside a candidate source
that still emitted arbitrary syllable strings. The human result rejects the
assumption that matching the surface rhythm of liked brands creates an
intentional name. The control arm's 8/12 rejection rate independently confirms
that reranking this whole-form source is not enough.

Do not tune the Phase 305 style distance, add literal character n-grams, swap
anchors, or reinterpret the two accepted prototype names as a stable positive
class. A genuinely distinct successor must change the candidate ontology:
start from a meaningful, brief-linked lexical concept and treat surface form as
a later constraint. It should be precision-first and may expose fewer than ten
research candidates; production capacity remains a later integration gate.
